About me

Two years of freelancing, one goal: make your brand impossible to scroll past.

I started as a self-taught web developer, then picked up 3D design to give my sites something most portfolios don't have — custom visuals instead of stock images. Today I build full websites end-to-end in Elementor, and design the 3D renders, product mockups, and hero graphics that go inside them.

Every project starts with your brand, not a template. I plan the concept, build the visuals, and develop the site so the two feel like one piece of work — not a design bolted onto a theme.
